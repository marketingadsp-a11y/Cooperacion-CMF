# **App Name**: Cooperacion CMF

## Core Features:

- Parent Record: Maintain a list of parents, with their contact information.
- Contribution Tracking: Log contributions from parents, noting the date and amount.
- Payment Status: Show which parents have paid and who are pending for each request.
- Cash Balance: Display the current cash balance.
- Expense Recording: Keep a record of all expenses, with descriptions and amounts.
- Financial Reports: Provide financial reports that outline income, expenses, and remaining balance.
- Contribution Reminders: Send automated reminders to parents who have yet to make a payment.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft green (#A7D1AB) to symbolize growth and community.
- Background color: Very light desaturated green (#F0F4EF).
- Accent color: Warm orange (#E5954A) for important actions and notifications, creating a sense of urgency.
- Font pairing: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for body and 'Playfair' (serif) for headlines.
- Use clear, representative icons for categories and actions.
- Design a clean, intuitive interface with an emphasis on readability.
- Use subtle transitions and feedback animations.
